I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University, working with Prof. Abhishek Halder. My research lies at the intersection of dynamics, control, optimization, autonomous and multi-agent systems, stochastic systems, and data-driven control.

I develop mathematical and computational methods for steering and inferring large-scale dynamical systems under uncertainty and partial information. My work draws on stochastic control, optimal transport, Schrödinger bridge methods, and mean-field models, with applications to collective and autonomous systems.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at UC Irvine, where I also received my Ph.D. in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Tryphon T. Georgiou.

Research

Dynamics, Control & Optimization

Control and optimization of dynamical systems with structural, distributional, and information constraints.

Autonomous & Multi-Agent Systems

Scalable methods for collective behavior, swarm dynamics, coordination, and distribution steering.

Learning-Based & Data-Driven Control

Inference and control from sparse, aggregate, or partial observations, with emphasis on physics-aware models.

Stochastic Systems & Decision-Making

Probabilistic formulations for uncertainty-aware control, stochastic dynamics, and path-space inference.
