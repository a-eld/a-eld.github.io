I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in Aerospace Engineering at Iowa State University, working with Prof. Abhishek Halder.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher at UC Irvine, where I also received my Ph.D. in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering under the supervision of Prof. Tryphon T. Georgiou.

Research [Selected Publications]

My research centers on three interconnected areas: interacting multi-agent systems, Schrödinger bridges, and optimal transport.

Interacting Multi-Agent Systems

Scalable inference and control of interacting populations and swarm dynamics, with emphasis on mean-field models and distribution steering.

Featured papers

  • A Generalized Sinkhorn Algorithm for Mean-Field Schrödinger Bridge IEEE Control Systems Letters, 10, 1087–1092 (2026); also presented at IEEE CDC.
  • Schrödinger Bridges over Kinetic Swarming Models Preprint, arXiv:2608.25281 (2026).

Schrödinger Bridges

Inference and control of stochastic dynamical systems from distributional, partial, and spatio-temporal observations.

Featured papers

  • Schrödinger's Control and Estimation Paradigm with Spatio-Temporal Distributions on Graphs IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, 70, 2466–2478 (2025).
  • Inferring Potential Landscapes: A Schrödinger Bridge Approach to Maximum Caliber Physical Review Research, 6, 033070 (2024).
More publications
  • Maximum Entropy Inference of Reaction-Diffusion Models The Journal of Chemical Physics, 162, 194104 (2025).
  • Inferring Evanescent Stochastic Dynamics from Marginal Distributions Journal of Systems Science & Complexity, 38(1), 129–149 (2025).
  • An Excursion onto Schrödinger's Bridges: Stochastic Flows with Spatio-Temporal Marginals IEEE Control Systems Letters, 8, 1138–1143 (2024); also presented at IEEE CDC, 2024.

Optimal Transport

Geometric and variational methods for steering dynamical systems and quantifying efficient control effort.

Featured papers

  • On the Isospectral Nature of Minimum-Shear Covariance Control IEEE Control Systems Letters, 10, 1171–1176 (2026); also presented at IEEE CDC.
  • Collective Steering: Tracer-Informed Dynamics IEEE Conference on Decision and Control (CDC), 3135–3141 (2025).
More publications
  • Minimizing Control Attention: The Linear Gauss-Markov Paradigm IEEE Transactions on Automatic Control, accepted/in press (2025).
